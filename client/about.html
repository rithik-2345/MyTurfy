Our Story

Built by players, for players

MyTurfy started with a simple frustration — booking a decent turf shouldn't take ten phone calls. So we built a better way.

How it started

A weekend match, ten phone calls, zero booked slots

It began the way most good ideas do — with a bad Saturday. A group of friends trying to book a football turf for a weekend match spent an hour calling five different venues, only to find two were fully booked, one didn't pick up, and the "confirmed" slot at the fourth turned out to be double-booked when they showed up.

That's the moment MyTurfy was born — a single place to see real availability, real pricing, and real reviews, for every turf, court, and lane in the city.

Our Goal

Make playing sports as easy as ordering food

We want booking a turf to take 30 seconds, not 30 minutes. And we want every venue owner — from a single neighborhood court to a multi-turf sports complex — to be able to fill their empty slots without needing a tech team.

Instant, honest availability

What you see is what's actually free. No calling to "double-check."

Fair pricing, no surprises

The price you see is the price you pay — no hidden convenience fees sprung on you at checkout.

A platform venue owners actually like

Simple tools, fast payouts, and a dashboard that doesn't need a manual to use.
