Our Story

Built by players, for players

MyTurfy started with a simple frustration — booking a decent turf shouldn't take ten phone calls. So we built a better way.

How it started

A weekend match, ten phone calls, zero booked slots

It began the way most good ideas do — with a bad Saturday. A group of friends trying to book a football turf for a weekend match spent an hour calling five different venues, only to find two were fully booked, one didn't pick up, and the "confirmed" slot at the fourth turned out to be double-booked when they showed up.

That's the moment MyTurfy was born — a single place to see real availability, real pricing, and real reviews, for every turf, court, and lane in the city.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cancellation & refund policy?

Cancellations are allowed at least 24 hours before your match slot time:

  • Within 1 hr of booking: 95% refund
  • Within 12 hrs of booking: 75% refund
  • Between 12 to 24 hrs of booking: 50% refund
  • After 24 hrs or <24h before match: 0% refund

How long are slots held during checkout?

To ensure fair access and prevent bot slot hoarding, solo bookings hold slots for 3 minutes, while split team bookings give your team 10 minutes to complete payments.

Can teammates pay a custom amount in split pay?

Yes! Teammates can pay their designated share, enter a custom amount (from ₹1 up to the full remaining balance), or clear the entire remaining balance for the team.

What happens if an account cancels repeatedly?

To prevent bot attacks and slot hoarding, any account that makes 10 consecutive cancellations is temporarily blocked from making cancellations for 24 hours.
