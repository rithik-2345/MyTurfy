Refund & Cancellation

Our Refund Policy

We believe in fair treatment for both players and venue owners. Read how our refund process works.

Last updated: July 2026

How Your Payment is Held

When you complete a booking on MyTurfy, your payment is collected and held securely by MyTurfy — it does not go directly to the venue owner at the time of booking. This is intentional and works in your favour.

You payPayment collected securely via Razorpay and held by MyTurfy
Slot time arrivesYour booking runs as planned at the venue
Payment released to ownerOnly after your slot has completed with no active refund request

This escrow-style flow means you have a real window to raise a dispute before any money reaches the venue owner.

Tiered Cancellation & Refund Schedule

Time slots are perishable sports inventory. Automatic cancellations are allowed strictly when requested at least 24 hours before the booked match time:

Cancellation Time WindowRefund PercentageDeductions & Fee Share
Within 2 Hours of booking95% Cash Refund5% MyTurfy platform fee (Must be ≥ 24h before match)
Between 2 to 12 Hours of booking75% Cash Refund15% MyTurfy fee + 10% Owner compensation (Must be ≥ 24h before match)
Between 12 to 24 Hours of booking50% Cash Refund30% MyTurfy fee + 20% Owner compensation (Must be ≥ 24h before match)
After 24 Hours of making booking0% RefundNon-refundable / Cancellation window passed
< 24 Hours before match slot0% RefundStrictly non-refundable (Perishable slot policy)

Owner Compensation: For cancellations made between 2 to 24 hours after booking, turf merchants receive up to 20% direct compensation fee to offset reserved inventory.

Bot Protection & Anti-Abuse: To prevent slot hoarding and bot attacks, solo booking slots are held for 3 minutes and split booking slots for 10 minutes. Any user account that makes 10 consecutive cancellations will be automatically blocked from making further cancellations for 24 hours.

Weather & Unplayable Court Policy

🌧️ Rain & Waterlogging

If an outdoor turf becomes waterlogged or unplayable due to heavy rain, or if a severe power outage occurs, customers receive a 100% Full Refund or a Free Slot Reschedule valid for 30 days once verified by venue support or MyTurfy Admin.

⚡ Venue Maintenance / Double Booking

If the venue owner cancels your booking due to emergency maintenance or court damage, you automatically receive an immediate 100% Full Refund back to your payment method.

Refund Approval Process

1
Submit Request via "My Bookings"

Log into your MyTurfy account, go to My Bookings, and click Cancel & Request Refund.

2
Instant Tier Calculation & Review

Our system calculates your exact refund percentage tier based on the time remaining before your match slot starts.

3
Admin Review & Approval

Your request is reviewed by MyTurfy Support Admin (myturfy@gmail.com). Once approved, the Razorpay refund is triggered automatically.

4
Payment Credit (5–7 Business Days)

Refunds are credited directly back to your original payment method (UPI / Card / NetBanking) within 5–7 business days.

Partner Payouts & Escrow Policy

MyTurfy holds all customer booking payments securely in escrow until the match slot time completes. Money is only disbursed to venue owners after the slot finishes with no unresolved refund request.

Partner Bank Details: Venue owners must register their Bank Account / UPI details in their Partner Dashboard to receive weekly automated payouts. If payout details are missing, earnings remain safely accumulated in escrow under their Partner Account balance.
