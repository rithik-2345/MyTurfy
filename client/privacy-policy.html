Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Here is exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Last updated: July 2026

Who We Are

MyTurfy ("we", "us", "our") is a sports venue booking platform operated in India. Our registered support contact is myturfy@gmail.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website and services.

What Data We Collect

DataWhy we collect it
Name, email, phone numberAccount creation, booking confirmation, OTP verification
Google profile (name, email, photo)When you use "Sign in with Google" — no password stored
Booking history (dates, times, amounts)To process your booking, send confirmations, handle refunds
Payment infoProcessed entirely by Razorpay — we never store card numbers or UPI PINs
Wishlist itemsTo save your favourite venues across sessions
Approximate location (city)To show relevant nearby venues — never precise GPS unless you explicitly allow it
Device / browser typeFor debugging and improving site performance

Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Venue owners — your name and phone number are shared when you make a booking so they can prepare for your visit.
  • Razorpay — our payment processor. They handle all payment data under their own Privacy Policy.
  • Google — if you use "Sign in with Google", subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Email service providers — to send you booking confirmations and OTP codes.
  • Law enforcement — only when required by applicable Indian law.

How We Protect Your Data

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we cannot read your password.
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption).
  • Authentication tokens expire after 7 days.
  • Payment data never touches our servers — it goes directly to Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.
  • Our MongoDB database is hosted on MongoDB Atlas with IP whitelisting and encrypted storage.

Your Rights

  • Access — you can view all your data by logging into your account.
  • Correction — update your name, phone, and city in account settings.
  • Deletion — email myturfy@gmail.com to request full account deletion. We will process it within 7 days and remove all personal data from our systems.
  • Data portability — you can request a copy of your booking history by emailing us.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this will log you out and prevent bookings from being saved.

Children's Privacy

MyTurfy is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us immediately at myturfy@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes by email or by displaying a notice on our website. Continued use of MyTurfy after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
